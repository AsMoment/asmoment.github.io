Privacy & Architecture

Privacy is stronger when it is
part of the architecture.

AsMoment is designed around separation: the social service coordinates people and sharing, while private content can remain encrypted in storage selected by the user.

01YOUR DEVICE

Create & encrypt

Private media is protected on your device before cloud storage.

→ encrypted content
02YOUR CLOUD

Store

↔ authorized access
03ASMOMENT

Connect & coordinate

The social layer supports relationships, communities, and sharing.

01Separation of roles

The social layer and the content layer do different jobs.

Traditional social platforms commonly combine social coordination and content custody. AsMoment is designed to separate those roles. The platform can help people discover, connect, organize, and share without requiring all private media to live permanently in platform-controlled storage.

This separation is central to AsMoment's privacy model—not an optional privacy mode added after the fact.
