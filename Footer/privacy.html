Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

POCIANET LLC (“POCIANET,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides AsMoment, a social platform designed to let people connect while giving them greater control over where their private content is stored. This Privacy Policy explains the information we collect, how we use and disclose it, and the choices available to you when you use AsMoment websites, applications, and related services (the “Services”).

Important distinction. AsMoment separates social-platform functions from content storage. Depending on the feature and your choices, private content may be encrypted on your device and stored in a cloud account you select, such as Google Drive, Dropbox, or Box. AsMoment still processes limited information needed to operate the service, such as account, routing, authorization, security, service metadata, and information you choose to make public. AsMoment is designed not to require a conventional centralized social graph describing users’ private relationships. AsMoment’s separation of the social service from supported private-content storage is intended to reduce unnecessary central collection of personal content and give users greater control over where that content is kept. This design can also reduce dependence on the lifecycle of a particular social application. Future features may allow user-controlled data to be used across additional services, including personal AI features, only subject to the user’s choices, permissions, and applicable privacy protections.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

We may collect account and profile information such as your name, username, email address, profile information, authentication information, and information you provide when you contact us. We may also process limited information necessary to enable communication, community participation, authorization, and sharing features. AsMoment is designed so that facilitating private communication does not require us to maintain a conventional centralized social graph describing the social meaning of users’ private relationships.

Using supported user-selected storage does not require you to operate or self-host the entire AsMoment service. The storage provider you choose remains subject to its own terms and privacy practices. AsMoment may support additional user-operated storage choices in the future.

Content and storage information

AsMoment may allow you to create or share text, photos, videos, files, and other content. Where a feature uses device-side encryption and your selected personal cloud, AsMoment is designed so that the private content is encrypted before storage and the AsMoment service does not need the plaintext content to provide storage. Some information necessary to locate, route, synchronize, authorize, or display content may still be processed by the Services.

Content you intentionally publish to public areas, such as Town Square, is public by design and should not be treated as private or confidential.

Cloud-provider information

If you connect a third-party storage provider, AsMoment may receive authorization tokens, account identifiers, file identifiers, storage status, and other information needed to connect the Services to that provider. Your use of the provider is also governed by that provider’s terms and privacy policy. We encourage you to review the permissions requested when connecting an account.

Technical and usage information

We may collect device, application, browser, IP address, diagnostic, crash, security, and usage information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services.

2. How we use information

We may use information to provide and maintain AsMoment; authenticate users; enable communications, communities, authorization, sharing controls, and content references; connect to user-selected storage; synchronize devices; provide support; detect abuse, fraud, or security incidents; understand service performance; improve features; communicate important service or policy changes; and comply with legal obligations.

3. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the Services; to a cloud provider when you direct AsMoment to connect to that provider; to other users according to the audience and permissions you select; when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security; or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not treat content you intentionally make public as private information. We do not sell private user content merely because it is stored or shared through AsMoment.

4. User-selected cloud storage

Google Drive, Dropbox, and Box are third-party services independent of POCIANET LLC. When you choose one of them, storage availability, retention, quotas, account security, and provider-side processing are also subject to your relationship with that provider. AsMoment may also offer limited AsMoment-managed storage for testing or selected features; such storage should not be understood as user-owned third-party cloud storage.

5. Encryption and security

AsMoment uses technical safeguards intended to protect information and supports device-side encryption for applicable private-content workflows. No security measure or networked service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your devices, credentials, recovery information, and connected cloud accounts.

End-to-end encryption and key protection

AsMoment uses end-to-end encryption for non-public user content. Encryption and decryption occur at authorized user endpoints. The AsMoment server does not store or access the encryption keys needed to decrypt that protected content.

If you do not explicitly choose your own encryption key, a random key is created for you and protected by splitting it into separate shares. The shares are stored separately, such as between your selected cloud storage and the AsMoment service, so that the AsMoment server alone does not hold the complete key.

You may instead choose your own encryption key. If you do, you are responsible for remembering and safeguarding it. AsMoment may provide tools that let you split that key into shares and place the shares in locations you select, such as separate email accounts, and later reconstruct the key from the required shares. The AsMoment server does not store or access the reconstructed encryption key.

Loss of a user-chosen key, or loss of enough recovery shares to reconstruct it, may make encrypted content unrecoverable. AsMoment cannot decrypt protected content on your behalf without the required key.

6. On-device storage, offline use, and notifications

After you have signed in, AsMoment may store encrypted content, cached information, settings, and pending updates locally on your device so that portions of the service can continue to work when you are offline. Changes made while offline may remain queued on your device until a connection is available and can then be synchronized with the service and, where applicable, your selected storage.

While you are offline, information from other users is limited to information that has already reached and been cached on your device. New information cannot be retrieved until connectivity is restored.

When AsMoment is closed or operating in the background, device and platform notification services may be used to alert you to time-sensitive activity such as chats. Notifications are intended to provide limited alert information rather than access to the detailed end-to-end encrypted private content. You generally need to open AsMoment to access the detailed information.

Locally cached information remains subject to the security of your device. Device operating systems may also control local storage, backups, notification delivery, and background processing according to their own settings and policies.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. Retention depends on the type of information and how it is used. Content stored in your personal cloud is also subject to the retention and deletion behavior of that provider. Deleting an AsMoment account may not automatically delete copies that other users lawfully received or content retained by a third-party provider under your instructions or its policies.

8. Your choices and rights

You may be able to update profile information, change sharing permissions, disconnect cloud providers, remove content, or delete your account through the Services. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or appeal. We will respond to valid requests as required by applicable law.

9. Children

AsMoment is intended for users age 16 and older. We do not knowingly permit children under 16 to create AsMoment accounts. If we learn that personal information has been collected through an account belonging to a person under 16, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law. Age and parental-consent requirements may vary by jurisdiction, and we may apply additional safeguards where required.

10. International use

If you use AsMoment from outside the United States, information processed by POCIANET LLC or its service providers may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions, subject to applicable legal requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AsMoment evolves. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be directed to POCIANET LLC at admin@asmoment.com. POCIANET LLC is an Iowa limited liability company.

This draft is intended to describe the current AsMoment design and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed against the implemented product, third-party integrations, and applicable law before broad public release.
