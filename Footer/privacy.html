Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

POCIANET LLC (“POCIANET,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides AsMoment, a social platform designed to let people connect while giving them greater control over where their private content is stored. This Privacy Policy explains the information we collect, how we use and disclose it, and the choices available to you when you use AsMoment websites, applications, and related services (the “Services”).

Important distinction. AsMoment separates social-platform functions from content storage. Depending on the feature and your choices, private content may be encrypted on your device and stored in a cloud account you select, such as Google Drive, Dropbox, or Box. AsMoment still processes information needed to operate the social service, such as account information, relationships, communities, permissions, service metadata, and information you choose to make public.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

We may collect account and profile information such as your name, username, email address, profile information, authentication information, and information you provide when you contact us. We also process information you provide to create connections, participate in communities, configure permissions, or otherwise use social features.

Content and storage information

AsMoment may allow you to create or share text, photos, videos, files, and other content. Where a feature uses device-side encryption and your selected personal cloud, AsMoment is designed so that the private content is encrypted before storage and the AsMoment service does not need the plaintext content to provide storage. Some information necessary to locate, route, synchronize, authorize, or display content may still be processed by the Services.

Content you intentionally publish to public areas, such as Town Square, is public by design and should not be treated as private or confidential.

Cloud-provider information

If you connect a third-party storage provider, AsMoment may receive authorization tokens, account identifiers, file identifiers, storage status, and other information needed to connect the Services to that provider. Your use of the provider is also governed by that provider’s terms and privacy policy. We encourage you to review the permissions requested when connecting an account.

Technical and usage information

We may collect device, application, browser, IP address, diagnostic, crash, security, and usage information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services.

2. How we use information

We may use information to provide and maintain AsMoment; authenticate users; manage relationships, communities, permissions, and content references; connect to user-selected storage; synchronize devices; provide support; detect abuse, fraud, or security incidents; understand service performance; improve features; communicate important service or policy changes; and comply with legal obligations.

3. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the Services; to a cloud provider when you direct AsMoment to connect to that provider; to other users according to the audience and permissions you select; when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security; or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not treat content you intentionally make public as private information. We do not sell private user content merely because it is stored or shared through AsMoment.

4. User-selected cloud storage

Google Drive, Dropbox, and Box are third-party services independent of POCIANET LLC. When you choose one of them, storage availability, retention, quotas, account security, and provider-side processing are also subject to your relationship with that provider. AsMoment may also offer limited AsMoment-managed storage for testing or selected features; such storage should not be understood as user-owned third-party cloud storage.

5. Encryption and security

AsMoment uses technical safeguards intended to protect information and supports device-side encryption for applicable private-content workflows. No security measure or networked service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your devices, credentials, recovery information, and connected cloud accounts.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. Retention depends on the type of information and how it is used. Content stored in your personal cloud is also subject to the retention and deletion behavior of that provider. Deleting an AsMoment account may not automatically delete copies that other users lawfully received or content retained by a third-party provider under your instructions or its policies.

7. Your choices and rights

You may be able to update profile information, change sharing permissions, disconnect cloud providers, remove content, or delete your account through the Services. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or appeal. We will respond to valid requests as required by applicable law.

8. Children

AsMoment is intended for users age 16 and older. We do not knowingly permit children under 16 to create AsMoment accounts. If we learn that personal information has been collected through an account belonging to a person under 16, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law. Age and parental-consent requirements may vary by jurisdiction, and we may apply additional safeguards where required.

9. International use

If you use AsMoment from outside the United States, information processed by POCIANET LLC or its service providers may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AsMoment evolves. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.

11. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be directed to POCIANET LLC at admin@asmoment.com. POCIANET LLC is an Iowa limited liability company.
