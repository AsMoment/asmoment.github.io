Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

AsMoment takes a different approach to social networking. Here are answers to the questions we expect most often during beta.

What is AsMoment?

AsMoment is a social platform built around a simple idea: the social service can help people connect without requiring all of their private content to live inside one company's storage. AsMoment provides communication and coordination mechanisms that let people connect and share, while applicable private content can be encrypted on your device and stored in a cloud account you select. AsMoment is designed not to require a conventional centralized social graph describing users’ private relationships.

How is AsMoment different from a conventional social network?

Most social platforms tightly couple the application and its content storage. AsMoment is designed to separate those roles. The platform facilitates communication and sharing, while private media can use device-side encryption and user-selected storage. AsMoment is designed not to maintain a conventional centralized social graph of users’ private relationships. Limited information may still be processed as necessary for account operation, routing, authorization, security, public features, and service reliability.

Why does AsMoment separate the social app from my content storage?

Separating the application from content storage gives users greater independence from the social platform. AsMoment can provide communication and sharing mechanisms without requiring all of your private photos, videos, and files to become part of a centralized social-media repository. For supported workflows, private content can remain encrypted and stored in a cloud account you choose.

What advantage do I get from keeping my content in my own cloud?

Think about the photos, videos, conversations, and memories you have accumulated over the years. They are part of your life—not just part of an app. Keeping supported private content in storage you control can give you more privacy and control, because your personal content does not have to become part of another social platform’s central collection. It can also give your digital memories greater continuity: if an app changes, shuts down, or you simply move on, your personal collection does not have to be tied to that app’s lifecycle. Over time, this approach can also make it easier to keep your digital life together instead of leaving pieces scattered across every social app you have used. Looking ahead, a long-term collection under your control could also provide a better foundation for personal AI—allowing AI, with your permission and within your privacy choices, to help organize, understand, and use your own information.

What does “Designed for a more personal social web” mean?

To us, “more personal” means putting the person—not the platform—closer to the center of their digital life. Your relationships remain yours. Your private content stays end-to-end encrypted and can be kept in storage you choose. Your memories do not have to be tied to one app or disappear when you stop using it. Over time, the same personal collection could support different applications and, with your permission, personal AI that works for you. AsMoment provides the communication that brings people together, but it is designed so that the platform does not need to own your private content or maintain a conventional social graph of your private relationships. The apps can change, while your people, your memories, and your data remain with you.

Do I need to run my own server to have more control over my data?

No. Traditionally, gaining more control over online data often meant self-hosting an entire service—setting up a server, maintaining software, managing security, and keeping everything running. AsMoment takes a different approach. By separating the social service from personal content storage, it aims to give you meaningful control over where supported private content lives without requiring you to operate an entire social platform yourself. You can use supported personal cloud storage while AsMoment provides the communication infrastructure. This offers a lower-cost, lower-complexity path to some of the data-control benefits people seek from self-hosting. Support for additional user-operated storage options may be added over time.

What does “Your Cloud. Your Content. Your Community.” mean?

Your Cloud means supported private content can be stored with a cloud provider you choose. Your Content means you retain ownership of content you create, subject to the rights of others and applicable law. Your Community means your social relationships belong to the people involved rather than becoming a social graph that AsMoment needs to own or understand.

Does AsMoment know who my friends and family are?

AsMoment is designed to facilitate communication without requiring the service to maintain a conventional centralized social graph of your private relationships. Your relationships are defined and understood by you and the people involved. Like a communication network, AsMoment provides mechanisms for people to communicate without needing to define or own the social meaning of those relationships. Limited routing, authorization, security, or operational metadata may still be processed when necessary to provide the service.

How does AsMoment protect my private content?

Your non-public content is end-to-end encrypted. Encryption and decryption happen at authorized user endpoints; the AsMoment server does not store or access the encryption keys needed to decrypt your private content. This means AsMoment can help deliver and coordinate sharing without being able to read the protected content itself.

How are my encryption keys protected?

You have two choices. By default, if you do not choose a key yourself, AsMoment creates a random encryption key and splits it into separate shares. The shares are kept separately—for example, between your cloud storage and the AsMoment service—so the AsMoment server alone never stores or has access to the complete encryption key. If you prefer, you can choose your own key. In that case, you are responsible for remembering and protecting it. AsMoment can help split your chosen key into shares that you place in locations you select, such as different email accounts, and can help recover the key later from the required shares. This gives you convenient protection by default, with the option for greater personal control.

Can I use AsMoment without an Internet connection?

Yes. Once you have signed in, AsMoment is designed to remain useful even when your Internet connection is unavailable. You can continue to access all of your spaces, view information that is already available on your device, and make updates while offline. AsMoment keeps those changes on your device and synchronizes them when you reconnect.

Being offline naturally has some limitations: you cannot receive new information from other people until you reconnect, so information from others is limited to what was previously cached on your device.

When AsMoment is closed or running in the background, your device may still receive notifications for time-sensitive communication, such as new chat activity. The notification alerts you that something has arrived without providing access to the detailed private content; open AsMoment to securely access the full information.

Do I own my data on AsMoment?

You retain ownership of content you create, subject to the rights of others and applicable law. AsMoment is designed around user control: supported private content can be stored in a cloud account you choose, and the service is designed not to require a centralized social graph describing your private relationships. AsMoment still processes limited information necessary to operate, secure, route, and maintain the service, and public content such as Town Square posts is public by design.

Where are my photos, videos, and files stored?

For supported private-content workflows, you can connect a supported personal cloud such as Google Drive, Dropbox, or Box. AsMoment may also provide limited AsMoment-managed storage for testing or selected features. The available choices may change during beta.

Can AsMoment see my private content?

AsMoment is designed so applicable private content can be encrypted on your device before it is stored. In those workflows, the service does not need the plaintext content for storage. AsMoment still processes information needed to operate the social service, and content you intentionally publish to public areas such as Town Square is public by design.

Why does AsMoment ask me to connect Google Drive, Dropbox, or Box?

The connection lets AsMoment place and retrieve content from the storage provider you choose. The permissions requested depend on the provider and feature. Your cloud account remains subject to that provider's own terms, privacy practices, quotas, and security controls.

What happens if I disconnect my cloud account?

Features that depend on that storage connection may no longer be able to retrieve or synchronize the affected content. Disconnecting AsMoment does not necessarily delete files already stored with the cloud provider; you remain responsible for managing those files and the provider account.

What are the four AsMoment spaces?

My Little Corner is for you. Family & Friends is for people you choose. My Communities supports groups you create or join. Town Square is the public space. The spaces make audience and sharing context more explicit.

Is Town Square private?

No. Town Square is intended for public sharing. Content posted there should be treated as public and may be viewed, copied, or reshared by others.

Which devices does AsMoment support?

AsMoment is being developed with Flutter for Android, iOS, Linux, macOS, and Windows. Feature availability and beta readiness may differ by platform.

Who can join the beta?

The beta is intended for people age 16 or older. Access may be limited while we test reliability, privacy controls, cloud integrations, and cross-platform behavior.

Does AsMoment use AI?

AI is a future direction for AsMoment rather than the defining feature of the current beta. Our goal is to explore useful personal AI that operates within privacy and permission boundaries established by the user.
