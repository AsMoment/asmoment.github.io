A different approach to social

Social should feel personal.
So should your data.

Photos, conversations, and memories are among our most personal digital possessions. Yet on conventional social platforms, they typically live inside infrastructure controlled by the platform.

AsMoment separates the social experience from the custody of your private content. You connect through AsMoment while your content can remain encrypted in cloud storage you choose.

Privacy by architecture

The platform connects you.
Your cloud holds your content.

AsMoment is designed so private media can be encrypted on your device before it is stored. The social layer coordinates relationships and sharing; your selected cloud stores the encrypted content.
